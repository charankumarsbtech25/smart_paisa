# SmartPaisa Legal Review & Indian Regulatory Checklist

## 1. Statutory Notice
All privacy policies, terms of service, and cookie disclosures drafted within SmartPaisa are product-specific drafts intended to establish privacy engineering controls. Prior to formal public incorporation and commercial rollout, they must be audited by qualified legal practitioners licensed by the Bar Council of India.

## 2. Applicable Indian Regulatory Frameworks

| Law / Regulation | Key Requisite | Implementation in SmartPaisa |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023** | Clear notice in plain language, verifiable consent, Data Principal right to erasure, correction, and grievance redressal | Plain-language Privacy Policy, self-service data erasure button, data export JSON archive, and Grievance Officer details. |
| **Section 9, DPDP Act 2023 (Children's Data)** | Restrictions on collecting children's data without parental consent | App is restricted to users aged 18 and older. Mandatory age confirmation notice on signup. |
| **Information Technology Act, 2000 & IT Rules 2011** | Publishing privacy policy, terms of use, and designated Grievance Officer with 48h acknowledgement | Published on dedicated `/terms`, `/privacy`, and `/help` routes with Bangalore jurisdiction placeholder. |
| **RBI Regulations (Payments & Lending)** | Prohibition against unlicensed credit dispensing or payment intermediation | Explicit non-advisory disclaimer stating that SmartPaisa does NOT provide loans, banking accounts, or securities advice. |
| **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020** | Prohibition of dark patterns, hidden pricing, and deceptive urgency | Clean cookie banner with equal prominence; no fake counters or subscriptions hidden in fine print. |
